# Misc — Product

## Identity

Misc is a catch-all knowledge space for topics that don't belong to personal life (Compass) or professional work (Exp2). It covers general-purpose subjects: tool configurations, learning notes, experiments, general knowledge, and any topic that doesn't fit elsewhere.

## Scope

- Tool configurations and setup guides (non-MCP)
- General learning notes and research
- Cross-domain topics (neither personal nor professional)
- Temporary subjects before they get their own space
- Any topic that doesn't clearly belong to personal or work
