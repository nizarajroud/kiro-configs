# 8 AWS Cloud Architect – Serverless & Integration Platform

Project : AWS Serverless Integration Platform – Équipe SALSA (Intégration Applicative AWS)

As an AWS Cloud Architect within the SALSA integration team at Beneva, I design and maintain the enterprise serverless 
platform that provides reusable infrastructure blueprints, CI/CD pipelines, and architectural guidelines enabling 
product teams to deploy event-driven workloads on AWS following organizational standards and security best practices.

Designed reusable Terraform shared modules and Backstage software templates on the internal Developer Portal, enabling 
product teams to self-service provision standardized AWS serverless infrastructure with built-in compliance and 
governance guardrails.

Engineered GitHub Actions reusable workflows for serverless CI/CD pipelines, standardizing build, test, and deployment 
processes across multiple product teams and environments (lab, dev, production).

Authored and maintained architectural guidelines and development standards for AWS serverless services (Lambda, API 
Gateway, Glue, EventBridge, AppSync, S3, CloudFront, SES), ensuring consistency and operational excellence across the 
organization.
